🐭 Mouse learns to find cheese

The mouse uses Q-learning: it keeps a score for every (square, direction) pair and nudges it toward “reward now + discounted best score of where I end up.” The heatmap shows how valuable it thinks each square is (its best score there). Each episode starts the mouse on a random square.
