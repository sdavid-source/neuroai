What the toggle is doing

Lifetime

Counts only the examples this individual or model met during the learning episode in question. Evolutionary history and pretraining are treated as free.

Total

Counts the whole experience budget: evolutionary shaping for organisms, pretraining for models, hand-engineered priors for symbolic systems.

Almost every point moves left when you switch, and the ones that move furthest — the digger wasp, Bayesian Program Learning, in-context learning — are exactly the ones that looked most impressive under lifetime accounting. LLM in-context learning is the crux: under lifetime accounting it sits alone in the empty corner; under total accounting it slides left and lands on top of pretraining. Same system, opposite conclusions, depending only on what you agree to count.

Three regions

Open problem: ARC-AGI has moved, and it moved in a way the map has to answer for

ARC-AGI is drawn here as a dashed target zone rather than a dot, because it marks where the corner would be if something got there. As of September 2026 the scores are contested and the leaderboards disagree, so treat every figure below as something to re-check rather than cite:

  • ARC Prize's own verified leaderboard — the one that also reports dollars — had the top verified commercial model at roughly 37.6% for $2.20 per task.
  • Third-party aggregators tracking the public frontier-API leaderboard report far higher, in the 85–92% band, on runs that are not cost-verified.
  • The compute-capped Kaggle track, which forbids internet APIs and caps spend near $50 a submission, was at 24% on the 2025 private set.
  • The human panel, over 400 participants, averages 60% at about $17 a task.

The spread between those rows is the finding. If frontier systems clear the human panel average while burning orders of magnitude more inference compute per task, the tradeoff this map argues for did not disappear — it relocated, from training examples to test-time compute. ARC Prize plots score against cost per task for exactly that reason, which is a close cousin of this figure with a third axis substituted in. Deciding whether test-time compute belongs on your map, as a third dimension or as a third accounting mode, is the strongest move available to this project.

Positions are arguments, not measurements. The axes are ordinal and the units are not commensurable across families — nobody can say a crow needed n examples in the same sense a network did. Every point is anchored to a specific study so the placement can be contested; low-confidence placements carry a dashed ring on the map and a note in the panel.

Before publishing, verify: trial counts and sample sizes in each cited study (these were drafted from memory), the cross-modal claim in the honeybee point, and the ARC-AGI figures above. On the total axis, the low end is heavily compressed — a wasp's evolutionary budget and a trillion-token pretraining run both land near zero, which the ordinal scale cannot separate.
